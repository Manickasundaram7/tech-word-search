How to play

1. Find a technology name hidden in the letters.
2. Click each letter in the word, in order. Words run vertically or diagonally, forwards or backwards; none are placed in a straight horizontal row.
3. Press Enter selection to check it.
4. Each correct word earns 1 mark. Each incorrect submission costs 1 attempt.
5. Find any 5 words. Click the timer display to start the 60-second countdown. You have 3 wrong attempts.

Tip: Search diagonally and vertically, in both directions. There are no horizontal words and no word bank.
